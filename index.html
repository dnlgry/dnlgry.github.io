How can counselling help?

Life can be complicated. Sometimes we find ourselves struggling with feelings, relationships or patterns we don't fully understand. Whether you're navigating anxiety, low mood, addiction, questions of identity or a difficult period of change or loss, you don't have to work through it alone.

Counselling provides a space to explore the challenges you're facing, and also the patterns, relationships and experiences that often lie beneath them. It isn't about imposing answers or telling you how you should live your life. Instead, we'll work together using curiosity, thoughtful reflection and, where helpful, gentle challenge to support you in finding your own way forward.

What to expect

Getting to know you

Our first sessions are an opportunity to explore what brings you to counselling, understand your experiences and circumstances, and identify what you'd like to achieve. Together, we'll develop a flexible treatment plan that reflects your needs and goals.

Working together

I generally suggest beginning with six weekly sessions, although the duration of counselling depends on your individual needs. We'll explore your thoughts, feelings, relationships and patterns, developing new perspectives and ways forward at a pace that feels right for you.

Reviewing our progress

We'll regularly review how things are going, discuss your progress and make space for your feedback. We can adjust our approach as your needs evolve, ensuring our work remains focused on what matters most to you.

A bit about me

Prior to undertaking integrative psychotherapy training in the UK and completing postgraduate counselling study in Australia, I had an extensive career in research, public policy, consulting, and thought leadership, underpinned by studies in physics, law, and public policy and management.

I also remain committed to my personal growth and professional development through regular personal therapy, clinical supervision, study, and training, and being actively involved with communities of practice.

My interests span psychology, philosophy, science, music, art and culture, united by a fascination with the human condition. This curiosity informs my therapeutic approach, particularly my interest in the unconscious, the search for meaning, and the complex interplay between our inner lives, relationships and the world around us.

My therapeutic approach

I bring warmth, therapeutic skill and broad life experience to my practice, alongside a genuine curiosity about people and the complexity of their inner worlds. My approach is relational, integrative and depth-oriented, attentive not only to what's happening in your life, but also to the patterns, experiences and unconscious influences that may shape it.

I won't presume to have the answers or tell you how to live. Instead, I'll work alongside you to explore your experiences, gently challenge familiar ways of thinking and open up new possibilities for understanding yourself and relating to others.

I believe meaningful change grows through a trusting therapeutic relationship, where we can approach difficult experiences with honesty, curiosity and compassion. Whether our work is brief and focused or longer and more exploratory, the relationship we build will be at its heart.

Acknowledgement of Country
