Hello there.

I'm Daniel, a registered counsellor offering in-person counselling in Brisbane and Melbourne, as well as online across Australia. My approach is person-centred, integrative and grounded in empathy, curiosity and respect.

I offer a confidential, non-judgemental space where you can speak openly, explore life's challenges and develop a deeper understanding of yourself, your relationships and what matters to you.

Thinking about counselling?

Are you feeling low, overwhelmed, stuck, disconnected, or caught in patterns that aren't working for you? Counselling offers a place to speak openly, make sense of things, and find a way forward.

We'll work together at a pace that feels right for you, exploring not only the difficulties you're experiencing, but also the patterns, relationships and experiences that may lie beneath them. I won't impose answers or tell you how you should live your life. Instead, I'll bring curiosity, thoughtful reflection and, where helpful, gentle challenge to support you in finding your own way forward.

A bit about me

Prior to studying integrative psychotherapy in the UK and completing postgraduate counselling training in Australia, I had an extensive international career in scientific research, government, consulting, and thought leadership. My studies span physics, law, public policy, Jungian psychology, and philosophy, and I remain committed to personal growth through ongoing therapy, study, training, and clinical supervision.

As a result, I bring relational warmth, therapeutic skill, and broad life experience to my practice, along with a deep interest in how people make sense of themselves and their experience of the world around them.

Why work with me?

Life can be complicated, and sometimes we find ourselves struggling with feelings, relationships or patterns we don't fully understand. Whether you're navigating anxiety, depression, addiction, questions of identity or a difficult period of change, you don't have to work through it alone.

I bring warmth, curiosity and genuine interest to our conversations. I won't presume to have the answers, but I'll work alongside you to explore what's happening in your life, make sense of your experiences and gently question the things that might be keeping you stuck.

I believe meaningful change grows from feeling understood, discovering new perspectives and developing a deeper relationship with ourselves. Whether our work is brief and focused or longer and more exploratory, the relationship we build will be at its heart.

What to expect from counselling

Getting to know you

Our first sessions are an opportunity to explore what brings you to counselling, understand your experiences and circumstances, and identify what you'd like to achieve. Together, we'll develop a flexible treatment plan that reflects your needs and goals.

Working together

I generally suggest beginning with six weekly sessions, although the duration of counselling depends on your individual needs. We'll explore your thoughts, feelings, relationships and patterns, developing new perspectives and ways forward at a pace that feels right for you.

Reviewing our progress

We'll regularly review how things are going, discuss your progress and make space for your feedback. We can adjust our approach as your needs evolve, ensuring our work remains focused on what matters most to you.
